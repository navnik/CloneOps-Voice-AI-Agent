# PARS Recruiter Call Analysis Prompt v36

Analyze the completed outbound screening call and write a concise, objective summary for a PARS recruiter.

Use only information stated or clearly confirmed during the call. Do not import an application answer unless the candidate confirmed it during the call. Do not infer qualifications from tone, accent, occupation, age assumptions, silence, or missing discussion.

Include:

- Candidate identity and whether the intended candidate was reached.
- Call outcome and whether the candidate remained interested.
- At-least-25 status.
- At-least-five-years driving-experience status and a brief description of relevant driving background.
- Tobacco or smoking status.
- Current work-authorization status.
- Valid driver's-license status, license type, and relevant endorsements only if discussed.
- Driving-record findings, including any confirmed hard disqualifier or issue requiring recruiter review.
- Smartphone access.
- Ability to complete at least four moves per month.
- Start availability, current location, preferred service area, and travel willingness.
- Whether the candidate understood and accepted the 1099 operational expectations.
- Whether the candidate understood that, if they advance, they may need to obtain and provide their own current MVR to PARS.
- Whether the candidate said they are willing and able to provide that MVR.
- Any discrepancy between application information and the live call.
- Any unanswered required topic.
- Recommended next step.

MVR terminology:

- Do not say the candidate consented to PARS pulling an MVR.
- Do not say PARS will obtain, pay for, or reimburse an MVR unless the call explicitly included approved information stating that.
- Describe the requirement as the candidate potentially needing to obtain and provide their own current MVR if they advance.

Decision language:

- Use “confirmed hard disqualifier” only when the call clearly established an approved hard requirement failure.
- Use “recruiter review” when information is incomplete, ambiguous, disputed, contradictory, or requires human judgment.
- When a hard disqualifier ended the call early, do not treat later unasked topics as a reason to change the outcome to recruiter review.
- If a topic was not discussed, say “Not confirmed.”
- Do not make unsupported hiring recommendations.

Keep the summary recruiter-ready and concise. End with one recommended next action.
